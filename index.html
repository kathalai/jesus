Who is Jesus Christ?

Jesus of Nazareth is the central figure of Christianity. Born in Bethlehem over two thousand years ago, His life, ministry, death, and resurrection transformed human history.

For Christians, Jesus is the Son of God, God incarnate, who came to offer redemption, hope, and eternal life to humanity through His sacrifice on the cross and His victory over death.

"I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."
John 14:6

Core Teachings of Jesus

Principles that redefined morality, love, and human relationships

Love God & Neighbor

Jesus taught that the greatest commandments are to love God with all one's heart and to love one's neighbor as oneself, extending love even to enemies.

Forgiveness & Mercy

He emphasized infinite forgiveness, urging followers to forgive others completely, reflecting God’s mercy and compassion toward humanity.

Humility & Service

Jesus inverted social hierarchies by teaching that true greatness comes from serving others: "The last shall be first, and the first last."

Jesus and the Catholic Church

The Catholic Church traces its founding directly to Jesus Christ, who appointed Saint Peter as the chief apostle, declaring:

"And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church..."
Matthew 16:18
  • Apostolic Succession: Continuation of spiritual authority through popes and bishops from Peter to the present day.
  • The Sacraments: Holy mysteries established by Christ, with the Holy Eucharist at the center of Catholic worship.
  • Universal Mission: "Catholic" means universal—bringing Christ's message of salvation to every corner of the earth.

His Global Influence

How Jesus Christ shaped human civilization, culture, and individual lives

Charity & Healthcare

Christ's command to care for the sick and poor led to the creation of the world's first public hospitals and major charitable networks.

Education & Art

The Church founded the modern university system and inspired some of history's greatest art, architecture, literature, and music.

Human Dignity

His message that every individual is created in the image of God laid the moral foundation for universal human rights and equality.

Global Faith

Today, over 2.3 billion Christians around the world—including 1.3 billion Catholics—find purpose, hope, and spiritual life in Him.
